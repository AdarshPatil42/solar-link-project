Company & International Mission

Accelerating Global Renewable Energy Deployment

SolarLink is a specialized B2B export catalog connecting certified solar photovoltaic, power electronics, and storage manufacturers with international project developers, EPC contractors, and distributors.

Certified Quality Governance

We only list equipment passing stringent third-party testing protocols (IEC 61215, IEC 61730, UL 1741, CE, and ISO 9001). Every shipped container is accompanied by individual module flash-test datasets and accredited lab reports.

Maritime Export Logistics

Specialized container booking, moisture-sealed vacuum packaging, and pre-cleared customs clearance documentation (Certificate of Origin, SABER, SONCAP, PVoC) across the Middle East, Europe, Africa, and Asia-Pacific.

Transparent Deal Execution

Real-time enquiry tracking (ENQ-YYYY-XXXXX), rapid 24-48h quotation turnaround, and flexible commercial negotiation mechanisms allow buyers to close clean procurement contracts with absolute predictability.

Step-by-Step Workflow

How It Works: 7-Step Export Process

From initial catalog discovery to arrival at your destination port.

01

1. Browse Certified Products

Explore our curated catalog of Tier-1 Solar PV Modules, Hybrid Inverters, Commercial Energy Storage, and Deep-Well Submersible Pumps.

02

2. Select Equipment & Verify Specs

Inspect dynamic EAV technical datasheets, certified test reports from TÜV/UL, and linear performance warranty terms.

03

3. Submit Project Enquiry / Bulk RFQ

Fill in required capacity, expected delivery timeline, destination port, and preferred Incoterm (FOB, CIF, DDP). An automated tracking number (ENQ-YYYY-XXXXX) is generated.

04

4. Technical Sales Review

A dedicated solar sales engineer reviews your electrical Bill of Quantities, checks container loading capacity, and models optimal ocean transport routes.

05

5. Receive Itemized Commercial Quotation

A formal quote (QT-YYYY-XXXXX) is issued with locked unit prices, marine insurance provisions, payment terms, and validity expiry.

06

6. Confirm Order & Contract Signing

Review and accept the quotation online, request fine-tuned adjustments, and execute bilateral commercial purchase agreements.

07

7. Export Packaging, Maritime Dispatch & Delivery

Goods are vacuum-sealed in reinforced export crates, loaded into ocean containers, and dispatched with a full set of certified trade documents (Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Bill of Lading).
