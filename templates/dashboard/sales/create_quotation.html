Incoterms 2020 Cheat Sheet

CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight):
Seller covers freight and marine cargo insurance to buyer's destination port. Buyer handles local customs.
FOB (Free On Board):
Seller delivers onto ship at loading port. Buyer arranges ocean freight, marine insurance, and customs.
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid):
Seller handles all costs including door delivery and import tariffs at destination.
