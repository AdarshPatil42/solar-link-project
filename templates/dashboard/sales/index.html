Logistics & Incoterms Guide

When building proforma quotations, ensure Incoterms 2020 definitions are clearly maintained:

  • CIF: Includes ocean freight and marine cargo insurance up to port of discharge.
  • FOB: Loading onto vessel at designated port; buyer assumes freight and customs.
  • DDP: Door-to-door delivery with destination import duties pre-cleared.
